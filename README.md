# Amazon Prime Video Content Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Amazon Prime Video Content Analysis Dashboard** designed to explore and analyze the platform's movie and TV show catalogue. The dashboard provides insights into content distribution, ratings, genres, countries, release trends, and the overall composition of available titles through interactive visualizations.

The objective is to transform raw streaming content data into meaningful insights that help understand Prime Video's content library and distribution patterns.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard highlights six major metrics:

- **Total Titles:** 9.655K
- **Total Ratings:** 25
- **Total Genres:** 519
- **Total Directors:** 5.771K
- **Start Date:** 1920
- **End Date:** 2021

## Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

1. **Ratings by Total Shows:** Displays the distribution of movies and TV shows across different content ratings, such as 13+, 16+, ALL, 18+, and PG-13.
2. **Genres by Total Shows:** Identifies popular genres based on the number of available titles.
3. **Total Shows by Country:** Geographical representation of content distribution across different countries.
4. **Movies vs. TV Shows:** Donut chart comparing the proportion of movies and TV shows in the catalogue.
5. **Total Shows by Release Year:** Trend analysis illustrating how the number of movies and TV shows has changed over the years.
6. **Prime Video Branding:** Incorporates the platform's branding to maintain a visually consistent dashboard experience.

## Key Insights

- Movies account for approximately **84.67%** of the total content, while TV shows contribute **15.33%**.
- Drama is the most represented genre, with 986 titles.
- Comedy ranks second, with 536 titles.
- Content ratings such as 13+ and 16+ represent a significant portion of the catalogue.
- The release-year trend indicates a considerable increase in content during recent years.
- The geographical distribution highlights the international presence of Prime Video content.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Data Cleaning & Transformation** – Preparing and organizing data for analysis.
- **Data Visualization** – Charts, graphs, KPI cards, and geographical mapping.
- **Data Analysis** – Exploring content distribution and identifying trends.

## Project Objective

To develop an interactive business intelligence dashboard that transforms streaming platform data into actionable insights through effective data visualization and exploratory analysis.
